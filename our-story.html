A bakery that listens first

Charu Cakes is built on one simple idea: a celebration cake should feel like it was made for you, because it was. Every cake begins with a conversation about who it is for, what they love and what the day means.

From there we bake to order, finish each cake by hand and pack it carefully, so it arrives looking exactly as we left it. No shelves, no shortcuts, and no two cakes the same.

Alongside our cakes, we stock the toppers and baking tools we trust ourselves, so you can decorate at home with the same confidence.

Every cake is a small promise: it will look the way we described it, and taste even better.
