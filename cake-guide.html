Storing your cake

  • Keep it in the fridge until 30 to 45 minutes before serving.
  • Keep it away from sun, heat and open windows.
  • Fresh cream cakes are best eaten within 24 hours.
  • Cover any leftovers and refrigerate them.

Serving and cutting

  • Take toppers, picks and any non-edible decor off before cutting.
  • Use a long, sharp knife dipped in hot water and wiped dry between cuts.
  • Cut tiered cakes from the top tier down, and remove the supports first.

Carrying it home

  • Keep the box flat. Never tilt it or hold it on your lap.
  • Place it on a non-slip mat on the car floor or boot.
  • Switch on the air conditioning on warm days and drive smoothly.

Eggless and allergies

Every flavour can be made eggless. Cakes can contain wheat, milk and nuts, so please tell us about any allergies when you order and we will advise you.

What is edible?

Piped flowers, buttercream and pearls are edible. Real or artificial flowers, ribbons, feathers and acrylic toppers are decoration only. We always tell you what to remove before serving.

Cake questions, answered

How do I know what size to order?

Use the size calculator above. As a rule, plan about 100 to 150 g of cake per guest, and go one size up if you have big appetites or want leftovers.

Can I change the flavour or filling?

Yes. Choose any flavour from our list. Tell us your choice when you order, and ask for eggless if you need it.

Can I pick up my cake, or do you deliver?

Both. You can collect it fresh, or we can deliver it. Delivery charges depend on your location and are shared when we confirm your order.

How long does a cake stay fresh?

Keep it refrigerated. Fresh cream cakes are best within 24 hours, and other cakes may keep a little longer. Bring it to room temperature for 30 to 45 minutes before serving.

Can you match a cake to my theme or outfit?

Yes. Share colours, a photo or a theme, and we will match the mood. You can also use the custom order builder.

How do I pay?

An advance payment books your date, and the balance is paid before delivery. We share the payment details on WhatsApp.
