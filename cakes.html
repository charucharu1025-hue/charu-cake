How big should a tiered cake be?

As a rule, plan about 100 to 150 g of cake per guest. Extra tiers add height as well as servings, so a big guest list does not need a huge cake table.

1 tier, 1 to 2 kg8 to 20 people
2 tiers, 3 to 4 kg30 to 40 people
3 tiers, 5 to 7 kg50 to 70 people

Not sure? Tell us your guest count and we will suggest a size.

Cake questions, answered

How far ahead should I book a tiered or wedding cake?

Please book at least 5 days ahead, and earlier for wedding season weekends. Single-tier cakes need 2 days.

Can you copy a design from a photo?

Yes. Send us the photo and we will match the style, colours and mood. Small differences in hand-piped details are natural, because every cake is made by hand.

Are the flowers, feathers and toppers edible?

Piped flowers and pearls are edible. Real or artificial flowers, ribbons, feathers and acrylic toppers are decoration only. We tell you exactly what to remove before serving.

Can I change the colour or flavour?

Yes. Use the cake studio above to try colours and finishes, then send us your choice. Any flavour from our list is available, and eggless on request.

How are tiered cakes delivered?

We box and transport them carefully. Delivery and setup details depend on your venue and the cake size, and we confirm them when we confirm your order.

How is the price decided?

By size, number of tiers and how detailed the decoration is. The prices on this page are starting prices, and we confirm the final amount on WhatsApp before you pay.

Don't see your design?

Send us a photo or just describe the idea. We will tell you what is possible, and what it will cost.
