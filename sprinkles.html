How much do I need?

12 cupcakesabout 20 to 30 g
1 kg cakeabout 30 to 50 g
2 kg cakeabout 50 to 100 g

A rough guide. Heavy coating or full cascades need more.

Storing and using

  • Keep jars sealed in a cool, dry place away from sunlight.
  • Add pearls while the buttercream is soft, or use a little frosting as glue.
  • Bright colours can bleed on very moist cream, so add them close to serving time.
  • Keep small pieces away from young children.

Sprinkles questions, answered

Are your pearls and sprinkles edible?

We tell you whether each item is edible or decoration only when you order. Please also check the pack, and ask us if you are unsure.

Can I get a custom colour mix?

Yes. Design a blend above, or message us the colours you want. We will confirm what is possible and the price.

How much should I buy for my cake?

As a rough guide, about 30 to 50 g for a 1 kg cake and 20 to 30 g for 12 cupcakes. Tell us your design and we will suggest a quantity.

Do the colours run on cream?

Some bright colours can bleed on very moist frosting. Add them close to serving time, or on firmer buttercream.

Do you deliver?

Yes. Delivery charges depend on your location and are shared when we confirm your order.

What if my order arrives damaged?

Message us within 24 hours with a photo and we will make it right.
