What can we customise?

Almost everything. Here is what you can decide.

Size and tiers

From a small 500 g cake to multi-tier cakes for a big guest list. Tell us how many guests and we suggest the size.

Flavour and filling

Pick any flavour from our list, and ask for eggless. Seasonal flavours are available too.

Colours and theme

Match your party palette, an outfit or the venue. Share a photo and we match the mood.

Toppers and names

Acrylic and card toppers with names, ages and messages, in gold, rose gold, silver or black.

Decoration style

Smooth and clean, pearls, piped blossoms, ruffles, flowers and character details.

Pickup or delivery

Collect it fresh, or have it delivered. For tiered cakes, we confirm the setup details with you.

Custom order questions, answered

Can I send a reference photo?

Yes, and it helps a lot. Send your order on WhatsApp from the builder, then attach your photos in the same chat.

Can you copy a design exactly?

We recreate the look, colours and mood. Because every cake is made by hand, small differences in piping and detail are natural. If something needs a tweak, we will tell you.

How far ahead should I order?

Single-tier cakes need 2 days, kids' themed cakes 3 days, and tiered or wedding cakes 5 days or more. Custom toppers take 3 to 5 days.

Can I change my order after confirming?

Message us as early as you can. We will do our best to make the change, depending on how far along your order is.

Do you make eggless cakes?

Yes. Choose eggless in the builder, or tell us on WhatsApp. For other dietary needs, please ask us.

How do I pay?

An advance payment books your date, and the balance is paid before delivery. We share the payment details on WhatsApp.

How are tiered cakes delivered?

We box and transport them carefully. Delivery and setup details depend on your venue and the cake size, and we confirm them when we confirm your order.
