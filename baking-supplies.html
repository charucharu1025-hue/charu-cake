    Looking after your tools

    • Wash piping nozzles and bags right after use, and clean the tips with a brush.
    • Wash non-stick pans by hand and avoid metal tools on them.
    • Dry everything fully before storing, to prevent rust.
    • Keep nozzles together in a box so none get lost or bent.

    Supplies questions, answered

    Which kit is best for a beginner?

    The Home Baker Starter Collection and the Non-Stick Baking Pan Set are a good start. Try the kit finder above, or message us and we will suggest one for your budget.

    Can I buy items from a kit separately?

    Message us on WhatsApp with what you need. We will check availability and share the price.

    Can you help me choose nozzles?

    Yes. Tell us the design you want to pipe and we will suggest the right nozzles.

    How do I look after non-stick pans?

    Wash them by hand, avoid metal tools and dry them fully. Check the pack for the exact oven temperature limit.

    Do you deliver?

    Yes. Delivery charges depend on your location and the size of your order, and we share them when we confirm.

    What if my item arrives damaged or incorrect?
