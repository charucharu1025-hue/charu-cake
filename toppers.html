How to choose a topper

A few quick rules so your topper looks just right.

Choosing the size

500 g cake3 to 4 in wide
1 kg cake4 to 5 in wide
2 kg cake5 to 6 in wide
3 kg cake6 to 8 in wide

A good rule: the topper should be about half to two-thirds as wide as the cake.

Materials and finishes

  • Mirror gold
  • Rose gold
  • Silver
  • Black acrylic
  • Printed card
  • Pearls and ribbon
  • Paper fans
  • Mirror balls

Acrylic toppers can be wiped clean and reused. Card toppers are best used once.

Using your topper

  • Wipe it clean with a damp cloth before use.
  • Insert the stick toward the back of the cake, away from the edge.
  • Take it out before cutting and serving.
  • Keep card toppers dry, and small pieces away from young children.

Topper questions, answered

Can I get a topper with my own name or age?

Yes. Design one in the studio above, or message us the wording on WhatsApp. Custom toppers are made to order, so please give us 3 to 5 days.

Will the topper fit my cake?

Use the size guide above as a starting point, and tell us your cake size when you order. We will suggest the best width.

Are toppers edible?

No. Toppers are decoration only. Wipe them clean before use and take them out before cutting and serving.

Can I order a topper without a cake?

Yes. You can order toppers on their own for pickup or delivery.

Can I change the colour or wording?

Yes. Tell us what you would like on WhatsApp and we will confirm what is possible before we start.

Do you deliver?

Yes. Delivery charges depend on your location, and we share them when we confirm your order.

What if my topper arrives damaged?

Message us within 24 hours with a photo and we will make it right.

Can't find your topper?

Send us a photo or the exact words you want. We will tell you what is possible, and what it will cost.
